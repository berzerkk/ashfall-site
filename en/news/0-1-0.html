The first playable version: a campaign in the Verdeclat forest, three classes, eleven talent trees and the forge.

DownloadPatch notes

First steps in the ashes

A five-level campaign in the Verdeclat forest, a boss at the end of each, an endless arena and a nightmare level that rises when you finish the campaign.

Three classes, eleven gods

Ember Guardian, Arcanist and Ranger, each with a passive and an ascension tree, and eleven talent trees with major talents. Skills, supports and auras unlock for the whole account.

The forge

Normal, special and ultimate modifiers, one-click reforging, rerolling a single line or the whole item, and corruption.

Patch notes

Content

  • A 5-level campaign in the Verdeclat Forest, winding layouts with no dead ends, a boss at the end of each level.
  • Global nightmare level: finish the whole campaign to unlock the next tier (+16 monster levels).
  • Endless arena: endless waves, choice of starting wave, personal record.
  • 3 classes (Ember Guardian, Arcanist, Ranger) with a class passive and a branching ascension tree.
  • 11 talent trees (War, Knowledge, Hunt, Flames, Frost, Lightning, Afflictions, Bastion, Mana, Alchemy, Legions) with major talents at tiers 10 and 20.
  • Skills, supports and auras to unlock for the whole account, gems up to level 20.
  • Movement skills (Rush, Blink, Shadow Leap, Lunge) and damage over time skills (Toxic Cloud, Curse of Withering, Burning Totem).
  • Elixirs with charges earned by killing monsters, unlocked after the first map.
  • Bows, two-handed weapons, off-hand (dual wielding or shield), legendaries for every slot.

Combat

  • Armour, evasion, block, resistances (max 60%, can be raised) and energy shield with a recharge delay and speed.
  • Skill slots unlocked over the levels, auras from level 20.

Forge

  • Normal, special and ultimate modifiers (ultimates from ilvl 40), tiers limited by item level.
  • One-click reforging, rerolling one line or the whole item, corruption.

Interface

  • “Geometric Forge” interface, vector icons in 5 styles to choose from.
  • Detailed tooltips for skills (current and next level) and items.
  • Options: sound, display, game, controls, what's new.
  • Automatic update at startup.
