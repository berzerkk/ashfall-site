Fire rethought

An enemy can burn up to four times at once, each ignite with its own damage. Hitting an ignited enemy spreads the fire to its neighbour, Blaze leaves burning ground and the Fireball can come back to you.

Patch notes

Fire

  • Multiple ignites: an enemy can burn several times at once (up to 4), each ignite dealing its own damage. Three ways to get more: the Double Ignite support, the Pyre class node and some legendaries.
  • New Stoked Burn support: +60% ignite damage for the linked skill.
  • Spread: hitting an already ignited enemy with a fire spell spreads the fire to its neighbour.
  • Blaze finally works: area fire spells leave a blaze on the ground that burns and ignites enemies.
  • Contagious Heat: a burning enemy can ignite its neighbour (no longer a duplicate of Wildfire).
  • Overheat: the gauge empties during the effect and then starts again from zero (before, a single spell was enough to restart it), and the bonus is shown with the other effects.
  • Fireball slower and with a shorter range. With Return, it explodes on impact or at the end of its flight, then comes back to you; if it hits an enemy on the way back, it explodes again. Pierce makes it go through.
  • Deep Burn weakened (+8% instead of +15%).
  • One word everywhere: you ignite an enemy.

Spells and skills

  • Chain your spells: every skill waits for its own cast time, but you can cast another spell, teleport or drink an elixir in the meantime. No more Meteor blocking your Blink!
  • Swift Archer no longer applies to spells; new Swift Sorcerer support (+30% cast speed for projectile spells).
  • Meteor: the warning area exactly matches the area hit, and the burning ground is clearly visible, including in boss arenas.
  • Brand new burning ground (Meteor, Blaze, Trail of Fire); several overlapping areas do not deal more damage.
  • Sprint elixir: +30% speed (instead of 45%). Rage elixir: no more movement speed. Potent Brew: +20% effect, without changing the duration.

Talents

  • Every node gives one strong effect and one small one, no more four-line nodes.
  • No more four-node paths: paths of two or three nodes, more of them, with 34 new nodes across the eleven god trees.

Music and ambience

  • Real composed music: a theme per act, plus the refuge, the arena and the bosses. In zones, the music gives way to quiet moments.
  • Recorded ambiences: birds in the forest, desert wind, the rumble of the volcano, waves and gulls on the coast and at the refuge.
  • Music and ambience change correctly from one map to another (act I no longer plays the beach).

Interface

  • A compact target frame, with ailments shown below; it no longer flickers.
  • An energy shield bar in a clear blue, with no stray line when it is full.
  • Clicking a skill or support slot puts the cursor straight into the search.
  • No more “Support unlocked” message when opening a slot with energy.
  • The character sheet shows the chance to ignite, ignite damage and the number of simultaneous ignites.
