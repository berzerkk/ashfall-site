Version 0.6.4 · November 8, 2026

Cemetery, catacombs and a new forge

The Verdeclat forest remade, a cemetery in the rain, bone catacombs, auto-crafting at the forge and a campaign up to level 55.

DownloadPatch notes

The Verdeclat forest has been completely remade, a cemetery in the rain and bone catacombs await you, the forge can now craft on its own and the campaign goes up to level 55. Have fun!

Verdeclat remade

The Forest Edge opens with a landing on a narrow beach, then the forest closes in up to a giant tree and its guardian. The Path of Bones winds past forgotten graves to the gate of the Morteval cemetery.

Cemetery and catacombs

Rows of headstones, enclosures, a mourning angel, then a great staircase beneath the crypt: the Morteval catacombs, lined with skulls and almost pitch black. Your lantern cuts through the shadows. Skeletons, wandering dead, archers and spectres guard the Gravedigger and the Lord of the Ossuaries.

The forge works on its own

Auto-craft targets one or more affixes with a minimum tier (“Strength T3 or better”) and runs real attempts until it gets there. The loot filter is remade, with a real preview of the loot on the ground and nine presets.

A tighter campaign

Level 1 at the start, level 55 on the very last map; acts II and III gain ten new layouts and you carry on along the path rather than through a portal.

Patch notes

Campaign

  • The Forest Edge remade: a landing on a narrow beach, tidal woods, a living forest that closes in, a giant tree to walk around and a guardian at its feet. Butterflies, swallows, fireflies, grass that bends under your feet.
  • Path of Bones (level 2) remade: a dense forest, an edge of forgotten graves, the gate of the Morteval cemetery, rows of headstones, enclosures, a mourning angel, then a hill and a crypt. The light turns grey as you go.
  • Morteval Catacombs (level 3), new: a great staircase goes down beneath the crypt, then galleries lined with skulls and femurs, almost pitch black: your lantern cuts through the shadows, the candles flicker. At the end, the Lord of the Ossuaries.
  • New monsters: cemetery skeletons, wandering dead, catacomb archers, ossuary wraiths; bosses the Gravedigger of Morteval and the Lord of the Ossuaries.
  • Acts II and III: ten new campaign layouts (dunes, necropolis, canyon, flows, forges…), solid mineral scenery, no more end portals: you carry on along the path.
  • The Wandering Lands keep the original maps.

Progression

  • Campaign recompressed: level 1 at the start, level 55 on the very last map. The Nightmares remain beyond.
  • World map: acts V to VII finally have their real layout (the climb of the Spire, the descent into the Depths, the approach to the Castle).
  • Slightly slower experience after level 20 (up to +25%).
  • Wand damage now applies to spells; no more imaginary bare-handed attack.

Forge

  • The selection stays on the item after a confirmation.
  • Auto-craft: choose one or more target affixes with a minimum tier (e.g. “Strength T3 or better” or “Dexterity T2”), and the forge runs real attempts every half second, with the sound, the same chances and the same costs, until it succeeds or you run out of materials.

Interface

  • Loot filter remade: rules on the left, an editor in the centre with grouped conditions, a real preview of the loot on the ground on the right; nine presets as cards; hidden loot can no longer be hovered or picked up (Alt shows it).
  • Talent tree choice arranged in columns (Physical · Melee, Magic · Elements, Shadow · Ranger, Defence · Support), without a scroll bar; the Back button works on the first choice.
  • Minimap: return and onward paths visible, locked in grey while the guardian lives.

Fixes

  • Terrain: loot no longer falls under the ground on hills and stairs; Shattering Leap, rushes, ground spells and monster leaps and charges follow the terrain.
  • Elixirs removed from the bar no longer come back by themselves.
  • Solid scenery: no more walking through rocks and trunks, rushes included.
