Living monsters, fearsome bosses and out into daylight

The monsters of the first levels move like real creatures, every boss has its own attacks and the catacombs lead out into the open.

DownloadPatch notes

The monsters of the first three levels finally move like real creatures, every boss has its own attacks and the catacombs lead out into the open. Have fun!

Living monsters

The wolf, the crab, the goblins, the skeletons, the wandering dead and the spectres have been animated from scratch: feet stay planted on the ground and every creature has its own gait. The wolf prowls at a walk, then gallops when it hunts, the crab walks sideways, the wandering dead limp with arms outstretched and the skeletons' skulls roll on the ground when they die.

Fearsome bosses

Grumm tears out a boulder to throw at you, the Gravedigger raises skeleton hands under your feet and releases the souls of his lantern, the Lord of the Ossuaries spins his scythes and closes a cage of bone spikes around you. Every attack is telegraphed on the ground before it strikes.

Out into daylight

Past the last door of the Morteval catacombs, an earthen tunnel climbs towards the light: you glimpse the surface as if leaving a burrow, then step out into a sunlit meadow.

Patch notes

Monsters

  • Animations fully remade for the wolf, the crab, the goblins, the skeletons, the wandering dead and the spectres: feet that stay planted on the ground, real joints, distinct gaits depending on whether the creature wanders, hunts or attacks.
  • Wolf: real canine legs, a four-beat walk when it prowls, a gallop when it hunts; it crouches before leaping and bites while shaking its head.
  • Crab remade: a serrated shell, eyes on stalks, a big claw with a moving finger, and it walks sideways.
  • Skeletons: a stiff walk, a jerking head, dangling arms, a sword held for real; when they die, they collapse and the skull rolls on the ground.
  • Wandering dead: they limp, dragging a leg, arms reaching out to you.
  • Ossuary Wraith redrawn: it floats, its tattered robe ripples and trails behind it, a skull and glowing eyes under the hood, long claws; it is translucent.

Bosses

  • Grumm, King of the Trolls: moss, mushrooms and a young tree on his back; he tears out a boulder to throw at you and makes roots burst out in three furrows.
  • The Gravedigger: candles and a raven on his coffin; skeleton hands burst out under your feet, the souls of his lantern hunt you down, and he flings earth with his shovel.
  • The Lord of the Ossuaries: bone wings and a soul flame; a whirlwind of scythes, a cage of bone spikes that closes around you and a spiral of souls.
  • Every attack is telegraphed on the ground before it strikes.

Morteval catacombs

  • The bone walls follow the galleries, with no gaps at the corners; the staircases have their walls and are lit by braziers.
  • New exit: past the last door, an earthen tunnel climbs towards the light; you glimpse the surface as if leaving a burrow, then step out into a sunlit meadow.

Fixes

  • The catacomb stairs are no longer black.
