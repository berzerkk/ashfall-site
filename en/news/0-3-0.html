Version 0.3.0 · October 30, 2026

Lands, legendaries and a visual overhaul

Eight playable classes, thirty more legendaries, the Tree of the Lands and a new main menu.

DownloadPatch notes

Eight classes

Ember Guardian, Arcanist, Ranger, Summoner, Pyromancer, Frostweaver, Stormcaller and Plaguebearer, each with its mechanic and class tree, and characters that are finally animated.

Legendaries and Lands

48 legendaries across four tiers, from the common T4 to the extremely rare T1, the Tree of the Lands, chests and map bonuses. The Elemental Beam becomes a persistent beam.

Menu and comfort

A new main menu and character selection, a single loading screen, a Codex of hoverable keywords and full graphics options.

Patch notes

World and characters

  • Eight playable classes: Ember Guardian, Arcanist, Ranger, Summoner, Pyromancer, Frostweaver, Stormcaller and Plaguebearer, with their mechanics and class trees.
  • Animated Quaternius characters for the eight classes, the Imp and the Pugnacious Goblin; lighter character models and weapons correctly attached to the hands.
  • Quaternius nature, village and craft scenery in the castle and the zones; first Quaternius sword, axe and shield looks.
  • A longer campaign, ten nightmare tiers and progressive zone levels. Tree of the Lands, chests and map bonuses.
  • Pre-computed levels and resource preloading to reduce hitches. Fixed duplicated models and leaks when loading pre-computed scenes.

Combat and progression

  • Elemental Beam remade: a persistent beam that follows the cursor without leaving old traces, distinct ice, fire and lightning effects, glyphs at the ends. A new Beam tag and a fix for damage bonuses per stack.
  • All Around and Return supports; fixes to totem projectiles, their limit and Deployment. Incompatible supports removed from Rain of Arrows.
  • Multiple hits: the first projectile of a cast deals full damage, the following ones 70% on the same target.
  • Energy on items of level 40 and above for supports; costs, compatibilities and weapon restrictions detailed.
  • Talent exclusivities removed. A new three-rank node in the Fire, Frost and Lightning trees: physical to element conversion, up to 100%. Fair stacking with supports.
  • Descriptions corrected, notably Barrage. Central notifications queued for unlocked skills, supports and slots, including when several levels are gained at once.

Items and forge

  • Thirty more legendaries: 48 in total, with four rarity tiers, from the common T4 to the extremely rare T1.
  • T4 legendaries reworked to offer real levelling alternatives to rares, with specialities and trade-offs.
  • Several legendaries' identities strengthened; tiers visible on new items as well as old ones. Fixed negative modifiers at generation.
  • Choice of the main skill for damage comparisons, implicit energy and re-energising at the forge.

Interface and comfort

  • A new main menu and character selection: previews loaded in the background, caching and class changes without rebuilding the screen.
  • Character switching removed in game: selection and creation only from the main menu.
  • A single real loading screen from the moment the character launches, with no intermediate screen.
  • A Codex of hoverable keywords and tooltips, available from the pause menu.
  • Options: frame limit from 30 to 360 with a slider, keyboard input and an unlimited mode; anti-aliasing, 3D resolution, bloom, loot visibility, buffs and life bars.
  • DEV tools: all Torments, +50 Tree of the Lands points and a catalogue of the 48 legendaries sorted by tier and slot, with direct generation of the chosen item.
  • Fixed the account reset and strengthened test isolation to protect player saves.
